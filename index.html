Build the resource pack?

Blockscope draws blocks with the game's own textures. Your browser downloads the Minecraft client jar from Mojang and the mods' jars from Modrinth, checks their hashes and keeps them in this browser for the next visit, with the skins of the file's player heads from Mojang's texture server. Nothing is uploaded.
